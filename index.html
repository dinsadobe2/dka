02 / The artist

Between the
hand & the pixel.

Dinesh Kumar is a fine and digital artist exploring the language of visual expression. Working across artistic mediums, his practice celebrates the relationship between observation, imagination, texture and color.

From the tactile character of traditional art to the open-ended possibilities of digital creation, each medium offers a different way to tell a visual story.
